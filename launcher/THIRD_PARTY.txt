Parts of the launcher that are not ours:

inc/WebView2.h, lib/WebView2Loader.dll
  From the Microsoft.Web.WebView2 SDK, Copyright (C) Microsoft Corporation. Distributed under the SDK's license,
  which allows redistributing the header and the loader dll with an application. The WebView2 runtime itself is not
  included; the launcher downloads Microsoft's own installer if the runtime is missing.

inc/EventToken.h
  Tiny stand-in for the system header of the same name (the compilers we use do not ship it). Ours.

ui/fonts/*.woff2
  Spectral (Production Type) and IBM Plex Sans / Mono (IBM), subsetted to Latin and Cyrillic.
  SIL Open Font License 1.1, see OFL-spectral.txt and OFL-plex.txt.
